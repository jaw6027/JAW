A little privacy.

We use the email address you provide only to confirm your request and send new nail art updates. You do not need an account. Your address is visible only to the owner and the email delivery provider, Resend. It is never shown publicly.

Your subscription begins after you confirm it by email. Each update includes an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops future updates. Contact the owner using their published social links to request deletion of your address.

We keep your address, consent time, confirmation status, and delivery status while needed to manage your subscription. Owner login uses an essential session cookie; forms use an essential security cookie. No advertising trackers are included.

If preview mode is shown, subscription submissions are not saved and no emails are sent. This notice is a starter policy; the owner should review it, add a contact method and retention period, and confirm local requirements before public launch.
